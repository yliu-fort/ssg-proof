\begin{theorem}[One evaluation never decides at a switchable start]\label{ed:one-query}
(One player.)  Let $M$ be a stopping harmonic system, $\sigma_1$ a Max strategy, $x_1:=\val_{M,\sigma_1}|_C$, $D$ the data of the single query $\sigma_1$, and let $v\in C$ be strictly switchable at $\sigma_1$, that is $A:=A_1(v,\overline{\sigma_1}(v))>\xi:=x_1(v)$.  Then $\xi<1$ and the system $M'$ obtained from $M$ by replacing the single row $z_{v,\overline{\sigma_1}(v)}$ by
\[ z' \;:=\;\Bigl(\tfrac{A-\xi}{1-\xi}\,,\;\tfrac{1-A}{1-\xi}\,e_v\Bigr) \]
lies in $K(D)$, is stopping, and satisfies $\val_{M',\sigma_1[v]}(v)=1$; hence $\sup\{\val^{\ast}_N(v):N\in K(D)\}=1$, attained.  In particular, if the start $v_0$ is itself strictly switchable at $\sigma_1$ and the queried system $M$ has $\val^{\ast}_M(v_0)<\tfrac12$, then $\Phi(D)=1\ge\tfrac12>\val^{\ast}_M(v_0)\ge\varphi(D)$: both bits occur in $K(D)$ and, by \Cref{ed:decide-criterion}, no evaluation algorithm decides $\val(v_0)\ge\tfrac12$ from that one query.  If instead $\sigma_1$ is closed then $\val^{\ast}=x_1$ in every member of $K(D)$ and one query decides.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proposition}[The extremal consistent systems are sparse]\label{ed:vertex}
(One player.)  For every Max strategy $\sigma$, both the supremum and the infimum of $\val_{M,\sigma}(v_0)$ over $M\in\prod_{(v,a)}K_{v,a}(D)$ are attained at systems each of whose rows is a \emph{vertex} of its fibre; by \Cref{ed:fibre}(2) such rows have at most $\rho(D)+1$ nonzero entries.  Consequently $\Phi(D)=\max_\sigma\sup_M\val_{M,\sigma}(v_0)$ is attained at a $(\rho(D)+1)$-sparse consistent system.  For $t=0$ the extremal systems are \emph{deterministic}: every row is $0$, $e_q$ or $e_{p_u}$; in particular $\Phi(\emptyset)=1$ and $\varphi(\emptyset)=0$.
\end{proposition}
